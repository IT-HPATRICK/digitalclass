About our school

Founded in 2005, we serve more than 800 students. Our teachers focus on strong foundations in mathematics, languages, science and technology, together with good character and teamwork.

Our mission

To give every learner the knowledge and values needed to succeed and serve the community.

Our vision

A school where every student is confident, curious and ready for the future.

Our values

Respect, honesty, hard work and responsibility.

Programs

Choose from the following levels and combinations.

Primary (P1–P6)

Literacy, numeracy and creative learning in a safe environment.

Lower secondary (S1–S3)

Broad curriculum with science labs and ICT lessons.

Upper secondary (S4–S6)

Specialised combinations that prepare students for university and careers.
